## 1. orangewen/Gen-nuScenes (Panacea)
- **Generator:** Panacea (CVPR'24). It is linked from github.com/wenyuqing/panacea ("We release our Gen-nuScenes dataset generated by Panacea"). The CVPR supplement reports 139,440 videos of 8 frames each, but that figure is the paper's, not my count. [V: repo text]
- **HF gating and license:** not gated, no license tag, empty README/card. [V] Because it is derived from nuScenes, nuScenes terms (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) presumably apply. [U]
- **Files:** `gen-nuscenes-val.tar.gz` 5.13 GB, `gen-nuscenes-train.tar.gz` 23.9 GB, plus infos pkls (val 127 MB, train 548 MB) and a 476 MB checkpoint. [V]
- **Val split, CAM_FRONT:** 4,976 clips × 8 JPEG frames at **512×256**, about 20 KB per frame. The tarball holds all 6 cameras. [V: full stream of the val tar] The infos have `version: v1.0-trainval` and cover **150 val scenes**, with 32–39 clips per scene. Each clip is anchored on a keyframe with `frame_idx` ≥ 7, i.e. sliding, heavily overlapping windows. [V]
- **fps:** not stated. Clips are keyframe-anchored, so presumably 8 keyframes at 2 Hz (about 3.5 s). [U] A pixel-correlation alignment against real keyframes was inconclusive.
- **Inspected:** I pulled and looked at the scene-0103 clips. They are plausible street scenes, low resolution, and the 2:1 aspect doesn't match nuScenes' 16:9.
- **Verdict: fallback only.** It has 8 frames rather than 16, about 2 Hz, 512×256 JPEG frames instead of video, and only about 600 non-overlapping windows. It fails the 16-frame spec.

## 3. Pairing plan (no scene overlap; official nuScenes splits: 700 train / 150 val)
- **Gen (eval):** worldbench `magicdrive` CAM_FRONT. Primary set: 150 gen0 clips, one per val scene, matched to gt. Full set: 600 clips (gen0–3). Repeat for drivedreamer2, opendwm, xscene and dreamforge as extra generators.
- **Real held-out:** 150 worldbench `gt` clips, which are the same val scenes and frames as the gen conditioning. Available now, no login.
  - To reach the pre-registered 200, add 50 more val windows from nuScenes trainval: 50 val scenes × 16 CAM_FRONT frames starting at keyframe 20, non-overlapping with the gt window. These need our own encode (see section 4).
  - Alternatively, run with n = 150 and log the deviation.
- **Real fit:** 500 nuScenes **train** scenes (out of 700) × 1 window: 16 consecutive CAM_FRONT frames (samples + sweeps, about 12 Hz) starting at the first keyframe. This mirrors the worldbench protocol. Scene overlap with val is zero by construction (`nuscenes.utils.splits`).
- **Frame-rate change:** this plan uses 16 frames at 12 Hz (1.25 s) to match the generators. It replaces the README's earlier note of about 6 Hz.
- **nuScenes trainval access:** the blobs answer HTTP 200 on the public CloudFront/S3 URLs with no login [V], e.g. `v1.0-trainval_meta.tgz` 0.46 GB and `v1.0-trainval01_blobs_camera.tgz` 17.6 GB. The **user should still accept the nuScenes terms of use (non-commercial)**.
  - Plan: download the meta file, then stream each camera blob and keep only the CAM_FRONT frames needed (16 per scene). That is about 176 GB of transfer, but kept files stay under 1 GB. The box has 81 GB free.

## 4. Domain control (re-encode everything)
Native formats differ: gen bitrates range 2.6–10.7 Mb/s, gt is 4.5 Mb/s, raw nuScenes is JPEG 1600×900, Cosmos is 1280×704 at 14 Mb/s 24 fps, and Panacea is 512×256 JPEG. All arms therefore go through `scripts/reencode.sh`:
`fps=12 → crop to 16:9 → scale 456×256 bicubic → libx264 High, CRF 23, preset medium, yuv420p, 16 frames`.
The model then resizes the short side to 224 and center-crops to 224 (`src/features.py`).

I tested the script on 8 clips: all 7 worldbench methods plus the raw nuScenes JPEGs (scene-0103). All give 456×256, 12 fps, 16 frames, 0.35–0.61 Mb/s. [V]

**Residual risk:** worldbench gt and gen were already H.264-encoded once, but real-fit clips made from raw JPEGs would be encoded only once by us. Mitigation: encode the fit clips twice, first at 1600×900 CRF 18 (to mimic the worldbench step), then through `reencode.sh`. Also run a control check: score AUROC(gt vs fit-style held-out), which should be about 0.5.
