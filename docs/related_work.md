## ⚠️ Closest prior: STALL is essentially our method
**Training-free Detection of Generated Videos via Spatial-Temporal Likelihoods (STALL)**. Omer Ben Hayun, Roy Betser, Meir Yossef Levi, Levi Kassel, Guy Gilboa (Technion). CVPR 2026, arXiv:2603.15026 (16 Mar 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15026 · code https://github.com/OmerBenHayun/STALL
- **What it does:** a zero-shot AI-generated video detector. It uses frozen **DINOv3** (ViT-L/16) per-frame embeddings, whitened with a calibration set of real videos (VATEX). It has a spatial branch (Gaussian log-likelihood of each frame, video score = max over frames) and a temporal branch (L2-normalised inter-frame differences, whitened, Gaussian log-likelihood, video score = min over transitions). Each branch score becomes a percentile against the calibration set, and the two are averaged. It needs no training and no generated samples. Evaluated on two public benchmarks plus their ComGenVid.
- **Overlap:** the core idea (real-only Gaussian likelihood on DINOv3 spatial features plus frame deltas, fused) is the same. We cannot claim the scoring rule as novel, and the old internal name "STALL" collided with theirs. **Ours is now called DriveLL (working name).**
- **How we differ:** (1) **Task:** they do misinformation-style real-vs-fake detection on open-domain video; we do *evaluation* of driving world models, meaning per-clip realism and generator ranking against FVD and human judgments. (2) **Features:** they use the global frame embedding (CLS/pooled); we use patch tokens with Ledoit-Wolf covariance, which allows patch-level heatmaps. (3) **Calibration:** they use a generic calibration set; we use an in-domain set (500 nuScenes train clips) and leave scenes out against held-out val. (4) **Fusion:** they use max/min plus percentiles; we use mean Mahalanobis plus z-sum. (5) **Our contributions:** a controlled-frame-timing protocol (the gt frame-duplicate confound), ranking agreement with WorldLens FVD (rho=1, 6 generators), sample-efficiency vs FVD, and corruption monotonicity.
- **Required:** the paper must cite STALL as the base method, run STALL's exact recipe as a baseline (their code and VATEX params are public), and rename ours (done: DriveLL). The baseline is in Block 7b, `results/block7b/`.

Image-domain ancestors from the same group:
- **Whitened CLIP as a Likelihood Surrogate of Images and Captions**. R. Betser, G. Gilboa et al., 2025, arXiv:2505.06934. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06934. It whitens CLIP embeddings so that the negative squared norm is a log-likelihood surrogate. This is the basis of STALL's spatial term.
- **General and Domain-Specific Zero-shot Detection of Generated Images via Conditional Likelihood (CLIDE)**. Betser, Gilboa et al., WACV 2026, arXiv:2512.05590. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05590. A conditional (local-subspace) likelihood for zero-shot detection of generated images. Relevant to "domain-specific calibration", which is our nuScenes-only fit.

## Positioning (suggested)
"A per-clip, reference-free-at-test-time realism metric for driving world models, (DriveLL) built on STALL-style spatio-temporal likelihoods. We adapt it to patch tokens with in-domain calibration and validate it *as an evaluation metric*: ranking agreement with FVD, sample efficiency vs FVD/JEDi, corruption monotonicity, and (planned) human 2AFC alignment." The novelty claim is the evaluation use and its validation, not the likelihood detector.
